Island 1.1: round 3, symmetric and read like a book

The four validated landmarks, each in a round border, on plain paper, read left to right then down, symmetric. Click one to see it whole, at 1/3 and at 1/20. 23 October 2026.

1

Four equal circles in a 2×2, read like a page: a → b, a return from b down to c, c → d.

2

A rosette: four identical petals around one centre, read in book order.

3

One great circle cut in four equal quarters, one landmark per quarter.

4

Two mirrored pairs of pebbles: rounded on the inside, squarer on the outside, a cross of paper between them.

5

Two lines of cells: on each line two round rooms joined by a dividing wall, like a cell splitting.
