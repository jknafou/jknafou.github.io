Draft: the TransCorpus-bio-fr logo, round 2 · design 3, «parallel lanes»

A squircle emblem split down the middle by a yellow translation gate: English blue on the left, translated lilac on the right. Three lanes of medical pages (DrBERT's red cross) queue in from the left edge, and in every lane a page is caught at the same instant, edge-on, turning over in the gate; on the right they queue out, each lane in another script (Cyrillic, Chinese, Arabic). The queues run off the emblem's edges (a stream with no end: massive), the lanes run side by side (parallel), white speed lines streak behind them (fast), and every shape is mirrored about the gate (symmetric); only the colour changes across it. Drawn entirely by transcorpus_logo_r2_3.py and r2_3_helpers.py.
