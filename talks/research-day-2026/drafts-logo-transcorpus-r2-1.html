Draft: the TransCorpus-bio-fr logo, round 2 · design 1, the radial burst

A page of medical text at the centre (DrBERT's red cross, its writing in the English blue of the source), and sixteen pages bursting out of it on a yellow badge, long and short rays in turn, all doing the same thing at the same moment: each leaves the centre white, with the source's blue lines, and past its hinge is caught turning over toward us, its lilac back widening and carrying a script — Latin, Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic, Devanagari, Chinese, opposite rays alike. A speed stroke past each short ray: they shoot outward. 8-fold symmetric; every shape mirror-symmetric too.

Mascot family look: black contours, flat fills with one shade band, white glints. Drawn entirely by transcorpus_logo_r2_1.py and r2_1_helpers.py, with round 1's drawn glyphs.
