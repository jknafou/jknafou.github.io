Draft: the TransCorpus-bio-fr logo, round 3 · design 2, the radial burst with a deck

A deck of medical pages at the centre, fanned like a hand of cards: its front page in the English blue of the source, DrBERT's red cross and a Latin A on it. Its sentences peel off it in every direction at once, each a blue strip with one ruled line; half-way out each twists over, narrowing to nothing and opening again, and comes out as a lilac arrow pointing away, carrying another script — Chinese, Cyrillic, Korean, Greek, Devanagari. Mirror-symmetric in every shape and script; no outer contour.

Mascot family look: black contours, flat fills with one shade band, white glints. Drawn entirely by transcorpus_logo_r3_2.py and r3_2_helpers.py, with round 1's drawn glyphs.
