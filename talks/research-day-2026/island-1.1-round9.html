Island 1.1: round 9, touching flat colour fields

No boxes, no gaps: the landmarks sit on flat pastel fields of the same shade that touch; depth only the characters' way (an offset darker copy, a white highlight blob), no gradients or soft shadows; arrows made of colour contrast; each shown beside island 2.1.
