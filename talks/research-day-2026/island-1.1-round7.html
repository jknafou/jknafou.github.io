Island 1.1: round 7, from your favourite pins

An agent studied your two favourite pins up close, measuring their tone-on-tone shades, and the new references you gave; then five designers each took a different creative direction, rounded shapes in shades of one hue on coloured grounds, some giving the island a meaning. Click one for whole, 1/3 and 1/20. 23 October 2026.

1

The open zipper: one rounded teal square split by an open zipper at the top, the two halves (English tools, few languages) closing into one below.

2

Minard's band: a rounded island in orange tones, a band that thins and widens like Minard's famous map as the data flows from English to the few languages and on to translation.

3

The dot-world: the island as one round planet made of hundreds of teal discs, each landmark in a smooth clearing among them.

4

Two voices, one voice: one big sand speech bubble holding four coloured speech bubbles whose tails meet at a central knot.

5

Nested pebble fields: one teal pebble with nine shades of the same hue, the landmarks in nested fields and a line rising through them.
