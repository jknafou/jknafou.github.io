Draft: the TransCorpus-bio-fr logo, three variants

The logo of the corpus: medical text being translated, into any language. A page with DrBERT's red cross is the medical data; a bold turning arrow is the translation; glyphs of several scripts (Latin, Cyrillic, Greek, Arabic, Devanagari, Chinese), drawn stroke by stroke, say any language, so there is no flag and no FR. In the mascot family's look: black contours, flat fills with one shade band, white glints, DrBERT's tones; blue is the source, lilac what translation produced. Drawn entirely by transcorpus_logo.py and corpus_helpers.py.

1 · the translate ring

A page of medical text (DrBERT's red cross, ruled lines) inside the translate ring: two bold arrows chasing round it, the blue one leaving a Latin A, the lilac one landing on a Chinese 文.

One medical page whose corner turns over: the front carries the source (a Latin A on blue), the turned corner, lilac, the translation (an Arabic ع); a yellow arrow turns with it.
