Island 2.2: round 2, a factory line

A sentence's journey as a factory line: one line through the tokenizer, then lines of tokens through pre-training, on to fine-tuning. Click one for whole, 1/3, 1/20 and beside the other islands. 23 October 2026.

1. The conveyor

One belt snakes back and forth in book order, parting into four belts for pre-training and merging again into the side of the fine-tuning board.

2. The production road

A winding road carries one line into the tokenizer, widens into four lanes through the ghost and TransBERT, then converges and climbs to fine-tuning.

3. The production line

A conveyor widens from one lane to four after the tokenizer, hairpins back to fork into the ghost and TransBERT, and carries the trained model on to fine-tuning.
