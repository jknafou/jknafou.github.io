ren_roar: six ways to show Ren weakened

Passage 472920__00068: “Rather than make him stronger in the dark side, as he had thought, it had weakened his spirit. He became unbalanced, and was more miserable and weak than before. The shock had been so profound, he did not register Chewbacca's roar of anguish nor Rey's scream of horror. Enraged by the loss of his best friend, Chewbacca fired a powerful shot from his Wookiee bowcaster and hit Ren in the left side [...]”

In all six, the roar and the scream travel to the faint circle of what Ren can sense and break at its edge, nothing gets in, and a “?” by him says he does not register them; his eyes stay still. They differ only in how he looks weakened. Each plays in a loop.
