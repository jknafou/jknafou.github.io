The title slide: six smaller emblems under the title

In all six the emblem stands under the title, narrower than it, its foot on the HES-SO mark's baseline; “tool calls” and “passages” run up the side of their parts; the bars rise one call at a time in pass 4's order while the passages roll by, the marked one (Ilum) coming to rest in front; at rest the answer's confidence breathes with its number following and the caret blinks after “jknafou”. B and C keep the passages rolling slowly at rest. Each slide plays, rests, and starts again. The HES-SO chip is drawn where the shell puts it.
